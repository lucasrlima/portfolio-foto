Cultural Heritage

In 2025, I photographed over 4,000 objects from Portuguese museum collections as part of a digitization project sponsored by Património Cultural IP.

The work involved museums and historical institutions across central Portugal, including Museu José Malhoa, Museu de Cerâmica, Mosteiro de Santa Clara-a-Velha and Museu José Manso.

The collections included paintings, sculpture, archaeological artifacts, ceramics and historical objects documented for preservation, research and digital access.
